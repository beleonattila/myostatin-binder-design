# 5JI1 — Apo GDF8

**The RFdiffusion target.** Human myostatin with no binding partner, so the ActRIIB epitope is fully exposed. This is the structure you clean and feed to the pipeline.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Title | Crystal structure of GDF8 (apo) |
| Deposited | 2017 (Walker et al., *BMC Biology* 15:19) |
| Resolution | 2.25 Å |
| Contents | Apo [GDF8](GDF8.md), mature growth-factor domain |
| Numbering | Mature-domain (UniProt O14793 residues ~268–376, "Coverage 31%") |
| Method | X-ray diffraction |

## Why apo is the right choice

RFdiffusion binder design needs a target whose surface at the intended epitope is **unobstructed**. Every non-apo GDF8 structure has something bound over the receptor sites:

- [3HH2](3HH2.md), 3SEK → follistatin / FSTL3 covering both epitopes
- 5F3B → antibody
- 6UMX → prodomain + Fab (latent form)

Apo-GDF8 (5JI1) is the ligand alone. The knuckle (type II / ActRIIB) epitope is solvent-exposed and available for a designed binder to be built against. That is precisely the geometry RFdiffusion expects: a clean target surface plus a hotspot list telling it where to make contact.

---

## Why 5JI1 over an AlphaFold2 model

You could use the AF2 model of O14793 as a target. Reasons to prefer 5JI1:

- **Experimental support.** Every atom position is backed by electron density, at a resolution (2.25 Å) good enough to trust sidechain placement at the epitope.
- **Real conformational state.** It's the actual apo conformation, including any receptor-relevant loop arrangements, rather than a prediction.
- **Cleaner validation lineage.** A 2017 deposition went through the modern OneDep validation pipeline — expect a far better [validation slider](../concepts/validation-metrics.md) than 3HH2.

Keep AF2 as a **fallback only** if 5JI1 has missing loops that happen to fall in your epitope. Note the paper reports apo-GDF8 conformational flexibility, so check the region you care about is actually resolved.

## Preparation checklist (Module 2)

1. Download the biological assembly from RCSB → `data/raw/`.
2. In [PyMOL](../tools/PyMOL.md):
   - Remove all `HETATM` (waters, cryoprotectants, ions).
   - Keep only the GDF8 chain(s) — decide monomer vs dimer target (see below).
   - Check for missing residues / alternate conformations (altloc) near the epitope.
3. Save clean `ATOM`-only PDB → `data/prepared/myostatin_target.pdb`.

**Monomer or dimer?** The active ligand is a dimer with two ActRIIB sites. For a first learning run, targeting a **single knuckle epitope on the dimer** (dimer present, hotspots on one monomer) is the cleanest, most physiological setup. Targeting an isolated monomer is simpler but less realistic. Decide deliberately and record the choice.

---

## Numbering — the thing that will bite you

5JI1 uses **mature-domain numbering** (the deposited fragment is precursor residues ~268–376). When you transfer hotspots from [6MAC](6MAC.md)/[GDF11](GDF11.md), both must be expressed in *this* scheme before they go into RFdiffusion's `ppi.hotspot_res`. Do the sequence alignment explicitly; don't eyeball residue numbers across structures. See [GDF8](GDF8.md#numbering-warning).
