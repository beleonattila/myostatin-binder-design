# 3HH2 — GDF8 : Follistatin-288 Complex

**Reference structure, not a target.** The classic myostatin crystal structure. Useful for cross-checking; disqualified as a RFdiffusion input on two independent grounds.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Title | Crystal structure of the myostatin:follistatin 288 complex |
| Deposited | 2009 |
| Contents | [GDF8](GDF8.md) dimer + [follistatin-288](follistatin-288.md) |
| Method | X-ray diffraction |
| Historical role | Long the reference model for the myostatin fold; used as MR search model for later structures (5JI1, pro-myostatin) |

---

## What it shows

Myostatin's mature dimer wrapped by follistatin-288, with follistatin's N-terminal domain rearranging to bind and its follistatin domains covering the receptor sites. It was the structure that established, at atomic resolution, that myostatin's pre-helix region closely resembles other TGF-β members and that follistatin neutralises by occluding both receptor epitopes.

It has been the workhorse reference for the myostatin field — later structures were often phased by molecular replacement using a 3HH2 monomer as the search model.

---

## Why it is NOT your RFdiffusion target — two independent reasons

**Reason 1 — the epitope is occupied.**
Follistatin-288 buries both the type I and type II receptor sites. The ActRIIB (knuckle) surface you want to design against is physically covered by follistatin's FSD domains. Even a geometrically perfect structure would be useless as a target here because the surface of interest isn't exposed. Use apo [5JI1](5JI1.md) instead.

**Reason 2 — validation quality.**
This is (almost certainly) the structure behind the poor wwPDB validation slider seen earlier in the project: clashscore ~44, sidechain outliers ~14%, elevated Rfree, Ramachandran outliers above modern norms. A 2009 structure predates the current OneDep refinement/validation pipeline. See [validation metrics](../concepts/validation-metrics.md) for how to read that slider. The upshot: **sidechain positions at the interface are unreliable**, which is exactly the information you'd need if you (wrongly) tried to read receptor contacts off it. Feeding clashing, poorly-refined geometry into RFdiffusion risks NaN losses or physically implausible conditioning.

---

## What it IS good for

- **Cross-check.** The GDF8 residues buried by follistatin's FSD domains overlap partially with the ActRIIB knuckle epitope. After you derive hotspots from [6MAC](6MAC.md), sanity-check that they fall roughly within the follistatin FSD footprint region rather than somewhere unrelated on the surface.
- **Fold familiarisation.** Loading 3HH2 in [PyMOL](../tools/PyMOL.md) is a fine way to first *see* the myostatin dimer, the fingers, the wrist helix, and how an antagonist engages it — just don't trust individual sidechain rotamers.

---

## Practical PyMOL note

If you do load it, identify the GDF8 vs follistatin chains before doing anything (the header lists chain composition). Do not accidentally extract a follistatin chain thinking it's the ligand.
