Pass = RMSD < 2 Å, mean pLDDT ≥ 70, TM ≥ 0.5, coverage ≥ 0.90. The endpoint returns a bare PDB with no pTM, so the documented pTM filter was replaced and stated rather than dropped. The coverage term is what earns its place: it cut design_5 from 7/8 to 3/8 by catching alignments over 30 of 52 residues that reported a flattering 0.55 Å.

Designability is a gate, not a score — a sequence that does not fold to its backbone has no interface. Interface area is the objective; one representative per backbone is a constraint. Every backbone keeps a sequence, design_5 included, so no axis had to lose.

Why step 5 is not optional

If the ProteinMPNN score predicted refold accuracy, this cloud would slope. It does not: ρ = −0.06. The best-scoring sequence of all 64, design_15_s4 (0.957), refolds at 1.65 Å; the best refold, design_12_s4 at 0.40 Å, ranks only 41st of 64 by score. Mean pLDDT does a little better (ρ = −0.31) but nowhere near enough to rank on.

How the panel was chosen

Step 6 · decided

The two axes never had to be reconciled by weighting them. Designability is a gate — a sequence that does not fold to its designed backbone has no interface, so it is applied first, pass/fail. Interface area is the objective among those that survive. 41 of the 58 self-consistent sequences clear the gate and all eight backbones keep at least one, so design_5 carries the widest interface into the panel on merit.
